User Guide

How to Read the Market Dashboard

Breadth, Sentiment & Sector Tabs

The dashboard is organized into three primary tabs. The Breadth tab shows the advance-decline ratio across all listed NSE stocks — a figure above 1.5 generally signals broad market participation in any rally.

The Sentiment tab aggregates multiple signals — volume trends, FII/DII activity, and put-call ratios — into a single composite score. Green means risk-on; red means institutional caution.

The Sector tab lets you isolate any of the 11 NSE sectors and see exactly which stocks within that sector are driving the move. Banking leading while IT lags? That's a cyclical signal.

  • Switch between NIFTY 50 and full NSE view instantly
  • Filter heatmap tiles by sector directly from the overview
  • Compare sector performance percentage bar charts side by side

Reading the Metric Cards

Each metric card in the dashboard header shows a live number, a directional arrow, and a 5-day sparkline. The colour coding is intentional — green doesn't just mean positive; the intensity of green reflects magnitude. A deep green card means significantly above average.

The NIFTY 50 card shows the index value, intraday change, and whether the move is broad (supported by breadth) or narrow (driven by 2–3 heavyweights). A narrow rally is flagged with an amber indicator.

Pro tip: Watch the Bank NIFTY / NIFTY 50 ratio. When Bank NIFTY outperforms, financials are leading the market — historically a bullish signal for broader market follow-through.

  • Colour intensity reflects magnitude, not just direction
  • Narrow vs broad rally flags on each index card
  • 5-day sparkline shows short-term trend at a glance
Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About the Market Dashboard

What is market breadth and why does it matter?

Market breadth measures how many stocks are participating in a market move. If NIFTY 50 rises 1% but only 15 of its 50 stocks are advancing, that's narrow breadth — a warning sign. True rallies are broad: 35+ stocks advancing together. Breadth analysis helps you distinguish genuine bull moves from index-level manipulations driven by 2–3 large-cap stocks. Equilytics shows advance-decline ratios for the full NSE universe, not just NIFTY 50 components.

What times are Indian stock markets open?

NSE and BSE regular trading sessions run from 9:15 AM to 3:30 PM IST, Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays. The pre-open session runs from 9:00 AM to 9:15 AM. Post-market trading (MTS) runs from 3:40 PM to 4:00 PM. Equilytics shows live data during market hours and the most recent closing data outside of those hours. All times are Indian Standard Time (UTC+5:30).

What is market sentiment and how is it calculated?

Market sentiment is a composite indicator that reflects the collective mood of market participants — essentially whether money is flowing into risk assets (equities) or out of them. On Equilytics, the sentiment score aggregates several signals: the advance-decline ratio, FII net buying or selling activity, options put-call ratios, and volume trends relative to the 20-day average. A score above 60 is bullish; below 40 is bearish. It's a directional guide, not a precise trading signal.
