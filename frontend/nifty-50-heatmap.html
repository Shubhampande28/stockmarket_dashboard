Reading the Equilytics Heatmap

Each tile represents one stock. Color shows direction; shade shows magnitude. It's the fastest pattern-recognition tool available for Indian equity markets.

🟩 Dark Green

Stock is up more than +3% — strong momentum. High-conviction session for bulls.

🟢 Light Green

Up +0.5% to +3%. Moderate gain — watch for continuation or sector confirmation.

⬜ Neutral

Change within ±0.5% of previous close. No clear directional signal today.

🔴 Light Red

Down −0.5% to −3%. Moderate decline — check sector breadth before acting.

🟥 Dark Red

Down more than −3% — significant selling pressure. High-risk session for longs.

Morning Scan

How Traders Use the Heatmap

At 9:15 AM, experienced traders open the NIFTY 50 heatmap for an instant read on market mood. If the top 10 tiles are all red, breadth is weak — be defensive. If sectors are splitting (IT green, Banking red), that's rotation, not broad weakness.

A heatmap glance every 30 minutes during the session catches sector shifts faster than any screener table. That's the edge a visual tool delivers.

Sector Rotation

Spotting Where Money Is Flowing

Institutional traders rotate — they don't move the whole market at once. If Banking tiles turn dark green while IT fades to neutral, money is moving out of IT into Banking. The heatmap shows this rotation in minutes, not hours.

FAQ

Heatmap Questions Answered

What does heatmap color mean?

Color shows % change from the previous close. Green = up; red = down. Darker = bigger move. Flat moves appear neutral gray.

How many stocks are in the heatmap?

NIFTY 50 view shows 50 blue-chips. "All NSE" shows 500+ stocks. Sector filters narrow it to 10–20 relevant names.

Can I filter by sector?

Yes. The Sector dropdown in the toolbar lets you isolate any of 8+ NSE sector groups instantly.

Is it free?

Completely free — no account, no payment, no sign-up. Open the heatmap at equilytics.in/heatmap.

When is data updated?

Every few minutes during NSE hours — 9:15 AM to 3:30 PM IST, Monday to Friday. End-of-day prices shown outside market hours.
