Data Sources

Equilytics combines market data, company financials, and research context to help visitors understand Indian stocks. Data may be delayed, cached, estimated, or unavailable during outages.

Sources

What Data Equilytics Uses

Market Data

Live or near-live stock quote data is used for heatmaps, top movers, sector breadth, and market snapshots.

Company Financials

Financial statement views may use public company data, exchange filings, Screener-style summaries, cached ratios, and linked annual reports where available.

News Context

News modules may summarize or link third-party market news sources for informational context.

Limitations

Always Verify Before Acting

Equilytics is a research starting point, not an official exchange record. Before making financial decisions, verify important figures with NSE, BSE, company filings, annual reports, and a SEBI-registered advisor.
