About

Know how the Indian market is feeling, in 60 seconds

Equilytics is a free, daily, plain-English market mood for Indian retail investors -- built from six real data signals, explained in full, every trading day.

Purpose

Why Equilytics exists

Most market-sentiment tools either hide their formula or describe it in jargon. The Equilytics Mood Index does neither: it's a 0-100 score built from six signals -- foreign investor (FII) flows, India VIX, Nifty 50 momentum, market breadth, 52-week highs vs lows, and gold vs Nifty -- each explained in plain English on the mood page, with the exact formula on the methodology page. Nothing is hidden, and nothing on the site tells you to buy or sell anything.

The Mood Index

One score, six signals, updated every 2 minutes during market hours and settled after each day's FII/DII data arrives.

The Daily Brief

An automatically written, fact-checked summary of what moved the market and why, published after every close.

Focus Lists

Four rule-based stock screens -- near 52-week highs/lows, volume shockers, steady climbers -- with the exact rule shown, not a stock tip.

Compliance

What Equilytics is not

Equilytics publishes market data and educational explanations. It is not registered with SEBI as an investment adviser or research analyst, and nothing on this site is a recommendation to buy or sell any security. Market investments carry risk.
