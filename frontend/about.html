About

Know how the Indian market is feeling, and look up any stock, in seconds

Equilytics is a free, no-login lookup for Indian equities -- search any NSE or BSE-listed company for its price, financials and news, and read the Equilytics Mood Index, a plain-English daily score built from six real data signals, explained in full.

Purpose

Why Equilytics exists

Most market-sentiment tools either hide their formula or describe it in jargon. The Equilytics Mood Index does neither: it's a 0-100 score built from six signals -- foreign investor (FII) flows, India VIX, Nifty 50 momentum, market breadth, 52-week highs vs lows, and gold vs Nifty -- each explained in plain English on the Market Mood page, with the exact formula on the methodology page. Nothing is hidden, and nothing on the site tells you to buy or sell anything.

Stock lookup

Search any NSE or BSE-listed company for its live price, financial statements and recent news.

The Mood Index

One score, six signals, updated every 2 minutes during market hours and settled after each day's FII/DII data arrives.

IPOs

Open, upcoming and listing-soon IPOs on NSE and BSE, with dates, pricing and subscription.

Compliance
