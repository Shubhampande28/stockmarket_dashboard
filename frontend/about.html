About

Indian Market Analytics Made Visual

Equilytics helps Indian market participants read stock momentum, sector rotation, market breadth, and company fundamentals from one clean dashboard.

Purpose

Why Equilytics Exists

Stock market data is often scattered across separate price tables, screeners, news pages, and charting tools. Equilytics brings the first layer of market understanding into one visual workspace: what is moving, where the strength is, and which stocks deserve deeper research.

Live Market View

Heatmaps and movers help visitors understand broad participation across NSE stocks instead of watching only headline indices.

Sector Context

Sector pages and filters make it easier to compare leadership, rotation, and breadth across Indian market groups.

Research Starting Point

Financials, ratios, news context, and educational pages help users move from price movement into better research.
