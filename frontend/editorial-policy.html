Editorial Policy

Equilytics publishes educational explainers and market-tool pages to help users understand Indian market data. Content is informational, not advisory.

Standards

How We Write Market Content

Educational First

Articles explain concepts, workflows, and dashboard usage. They do not recommend buying, selling, or holding securities.

Clear Limitations

Market data can be delayed or inaccurate. Content should be treated as a starting point for independent verification.

No Hidden Advice

Heatmap colors, rankings, and sector pages are descriptive signals, not investment calls.

Updates

Review and Corrections

Pages may be updated when product features, data sources, or market terminology changes. Material corrections are handled through the corrections policy.
