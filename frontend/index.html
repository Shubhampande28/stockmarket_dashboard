FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a stock market heatmap?
A color-coded grid where each tile is one stock — green means up, red means down versus the previous day's close. Color intensity shows the size of the move. Equilytics provides real-time heatmaps for NSE stocks covering NIFTY 50, Bank NIFTY, Midcap and sector indices.
Which indices does Equilytics cover?
NIFTY 50, Bank NIFTY, FIN NIFTY, NIFTY Midcap and SENSEX. Sector views include NIFTY Auto, Bank, Energy, FMCG, IT, Metal, Pharma and Realty.
How often is market data updated?
Data refreshes every few minutes during NSE and BSE trading hours — Monday to Friday, 9:15 AM to 3:30 PM IST. Quotes may carry a short delay. Always verify critical prices with the official NSE or BSE website.
Can I view company financial statements?
Yes. The Financials page lets you search any NSE-listed company and view P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, and ratios (P/E, ROE, ROCE). Data from Screener.in and official NSE filings.
Is Equilytics free?
Yes — completely free, no account required. All features including heatmap, market overview, gainers/losers, sector analysis, and company financials are available to every visitor.
Does Equilytics provide investment advice?
No. Everything on Equilytics is for informational and educational purposes only. Nothing constitutes investment advice or a buy/sell recommendation. Always consult a SEBI-registered advisor before making financial decisions.
