Best Free Indian Stock Market Dashboard Features to Look For

A good stock market dashboard should help you answer three questions quickly: what is moving, why it may be moving, and where to research next.

Live Heatmap

A heatmap gives a fast read of index strength, sector pressure, and heavyweight impact.

Top Movers

Gainers and losers show where the strongest price action is happening now.

Sector Pages

Sector views help compare banking, IT, pharma, auto, energy, metals, and other groups.

Company Financials

Price movement matters more when it can be checked against business quality and valuation.
