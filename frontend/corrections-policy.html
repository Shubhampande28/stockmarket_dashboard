Corrections Policy

Equilytics aims to correct material mistakes in educational content, source descriptions, and dashboard copy when they are identified.

Process

How to Report an Issue

When reporting a correction, include the page URL, stock symbol if relevant, the issue you found, the expected correction, and a source link where possible.

Market Data

Quote issues may come from upstream provider delays, market holidays, symbol changes, or temporary API failures.

Financial Data

Financial figures should be verified against official filings and annual reports before a correction is accepted.

Article Content

Conceptual or wording errors can be corrected directly in the relevant article or guide.
